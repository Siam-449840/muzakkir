Privacy Policy • Effective Date: September 10, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Muzakkir – Islamic Reminder ("Muzakkir", "we", "our"). Muzakkir is a purposeful spiritual companion built to bring Quranic verses and authentic prophetic traditions into your daily routine through scheduled notifications and floating reminder cards.

Core Privacy Promise: Muzakkir operates on an on-device local storage architecture. All Islamic texts, 10 language translations, reminder times, and bookmarks remain entirely on your phone. The app contains zero advertisements, zero third-party behavioral trackers, and requires no user registration.

2. Data Storage and Network Communication

A. On-Device Local Storage (Offline Data)

  • Spiritual Content: Over 500 curated Quran reminder units and thousands of Sahih Hadiths are embedded directly in the application bundle.
  • User Preferences: Reminder schedules, active times, selected translation language, and quiet hours are stored in private local app storage (AsyncStorage / SharedPreferences).
  • Personal Bookmarks & History: Verses and hadiths marked as favorite or read are stored strictly on your device.
  • No Account Required: We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or credentials.

B. Recitation Audio Streaming (Network Usage Disclosure)

When playing Quran recitations, audio is streamed directly from the trusted public Islamic CDN (https://everyayah.com) over HTTPS.

Like all internet web servers, the hosting CDN receives standard network connection metadata (your device IP address, user-agent, and requested audio file path) to fulfill audio requests and maintain infrastructure security. Muzakkir attaches no personal identification or tracking parameters to these requests.

3. Android Permissions & Justification

Permission Android Identifier Purpose in Muzakkir
Post Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays reminder alerts in the system tray at your configured times.
Exact Alarms SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Allows the reminder alarm to wake the device at exact user-set minutes (even during Doze mode).
Overlay Window SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Allows optional, temporary floating reminder cards over active apps for brief reflection.
Foreground Service FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Presents the floating overlay immediately upon the exact scheduled alarm firing outside the app.
Reboot Rescheduling RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores your active reminder alarms when your phone restarts.

4. Children's Privacy

Muzakkir complies with COPPA and Google Play Families guidelines. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, uninstalling Muzakkir or clearing app storage in Android Settings instantly and permanently deletes all your saved bookmarks, history, and preferences.
